\section{Ethical Considerations}
\label{sec:ethics}

We use public or research benchmark tasks and do not introduce private user data. CTF-Dojo runs only inside isolated benchmark containers and does not target real systems. Human steering is limited to source guidance, task-board edits, and credential decisions; humans do not label answers or choose solver branches. Released artifacts should preserve sandbox restrictions and exclude credentials or provider secrets.
