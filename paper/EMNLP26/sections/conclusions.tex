\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}

Existing auto-harness systems neither improve construction over time nor adapt to each incoming task. We address both gaps with a stateful multi-agent evolver that accumulates cross-cycle knowledge and a harness-tree router that selects a suitable branch at solve time. On three finite prefixes of unbounded deployment streams, the system outperforms all baselines and is the only one whose capability compounds over the stream.
