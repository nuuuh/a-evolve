\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}
\vspace{-0.35em}

Open-ended task streams expose three challenges for auto-harness deployment: unbounded task arrival, heterogeneous tasks, and non-stationarity. Adaptive Auto-Harness addresses them by pairing sustained harness construction with solve-time task adaptation. The decomposition into evolution loss and adaptation loss explains why a single repeatedly updated harness is insufficient: the system must construct missing capabilities from stream evidence while selecting a suitable specialized branch for each task. Across three streams, the experiments show complementarity: evolution builds capabilities, routing exploits specialization, and human steering supplies external signals absent from history.
