\begin{abstract}
Auto-harness systems such as A-Evolve, GEPA, and Meta-Harness automatically construct an LLM agent's harness (prompts, skills, \bing{memories}, tools, infrastructure) and report strong gains on static benchmarks. Real-world deployment, however, demands \emph{sustained agentic system optimization}: continual improvement of the harness as the agent operates on an unbounded stream of tasks. We identify three deployment dimensions that prior systems overlook and propose an analytical framework, a regret decomposition $\Levo + \Ladapt$, that derives two complementary operations: \emph{continual auto-harness} (a stateful multi-agent evolver with cross-cycle knowledge) and \emph{runtime adaptation} (a strategy-tree navigator that selects a task-relevant harness prior to solving), plus a human-in-the-loop channel for regimes outside prior experience. Our system outperforms A-Evolve, GEPA, and Meta-Harness on three real task streams. \zw{will revise in the end}
\end{abstract}
