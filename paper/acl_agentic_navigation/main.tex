\begin{abstract}
Auto-harness systems such as A-Evolve, GEPA, and Meta-Harness automatically construct an LLM agent's harness (prompts, skills, tools, infrastructure) and report strong gains on static benchmarks. Real deployment, however, demands \emph{continual self-improvement}: a sustained improvement of the harness as the agent operates on a long-running stream of tasks. We identify three deployment dimensions that prior systems overlook and propose an analytical framework, a regret decomposition $\Levo + \Ladapt$, that derives two complementary operations: \emph{continual auto-harness} (a stateful multi-agent evolver with cross-cycle knowledge) and \emph{solve-time adaptation} (a strategy-tree navigator that selects a task-relevant harness prior to solving), plus a human-in-the-loop channel for regimes outside prior experience. Our system outperforms A-Evolve, GEPA, and Meta-Harness on three real task streams.
\end{abstract}
